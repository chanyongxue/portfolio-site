## Emphasis on community building and gamification

CEO and Co-Founders of Elevate You aimed to bring community and gamification as part of the core experience within the app. To help with self-development, we delve into many ideas on how we can help users stay in the app and work towards their goals. In the community feature and the ability to share/view streaks, it brings together the core elements into the application.

## If I could go back in time, what would I do differently?

Because the project shifted project managers mid-way during the design phase, the transition of knowledge had slight barriers which required me to attend additional meetings with the new PM and CTO to help bridge any knowledge gaps. To aid in communications, I would’ve included more annotations to my work so there’s clear identifiers on features for the team.

<p class="kicker">Common Pain Point</p>

## No longer needing to click through 5 different steps to make 1 purchase.

It was a grueling process for Knowledge Coop’s end-users. With most of them being comprised of professionals in their late 30s to 50s, I needed to ensure my design solutions consider behavioral patterns they may already be accustomed to with other platforms that support shopping.

<p class="kicker">Shopping Cart</p>

## Powered with Stripe to manage all purchases & reducing purchase time by 90%!

No longer are users having to conduct separate purchases from Shopify and Stripe. Now, all coupon codes and shopping experiences are united in one system and journey. Coop+ users know exactly what they need to be licensed in and would routinely conduct CE annually so being able to make the purchase in less than 5 clicks simplifies their experience by a long shot!

<p class="kicker">Old Design / Experience</p>

## The original user experience only allowed one purchase at a time - not a fun time for Loan Officers that are licensed in 6+ states.

Users were required to open each product page and make each individual purchase, of which would take an average of 5 minutes to complete the purchases they need.

- **Disjointed Payment Systems**
  - Marketing site (Shopify) and Coop+ (Stripe) create challenges for both users and internal data tracking.
  - Users are unaware that discount codes are platform-specific, leading to failed purchases and increased support inquiries.
- **Lack of Multi-Course Purchase Feature on Coop+**
  - Users must make individual purchases for each course, causing inefficiencies and frustration, especially for those licensed in multiple states.
- **Suboptimal Shopping Experience**
  - Redundant steps in the marketing site’s purchasing flow (e.g., entering the same form details twice when purchasing PE courses).
  - Navigation challenges due to unclear differentiation between the two platforms.
